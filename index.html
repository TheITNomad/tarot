PRIVATE READINGS AND CANDLE WORK

For private candle work, I provide a personalized experience by sending you a video via email of me reciting your specific spell and lighting the candle on your behalf. This allows you to have a tangible connection to the work being done, and you can revisit the video whenever you feel called to.

Readings are done via email and can be especially beneficial because it gives you the space to fully absorb the message at your own pace. Instead of trying to remember everything in the moment, you have the entire reading written out for you to revisit whenever you feel called to.

This allows you to reflect more deeply, notice new insights over time, and connect different parts of the reading to situations as they unfold in your life. Many people find that messages continue to reveal new meaning the more they return to them, offering ongoing clarity and guidance long after the reading is received.

These extended readings include the opportunity to ask any follow-up questions, so you can gain deeper clarity and understanding.

A Gift Honed Over Decades

For over 35 years, I have walked the path between worlds reading the language of cards, stars, runes, and flame for those seeking clarity in love, life, and purpose. My gift was awakened early and has been refined through decades of dedicated practice and thousands of readings.

Married for nearly 40 years, I understand deeply the threads of relationships the joy, the complexity, the seasons of love. My readings carry not just mystical insight but the wisdom of lived experience. Whether you seek guidance on love, career, family, or your soul's greater journey, I am here to illuminate the path.

From the misty forests of the Pacific Northwest, I offer you a sacred space to receive the messages the universe has waiting for you.
